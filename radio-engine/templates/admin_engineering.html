Bitrate guide

96 kbps is fine for talk / podcasting. 128 kbps is the long-standing music radio default. 192–256 kbps sounds noticeably better; 320 maxes out MP3.

Crossfade guide

0 = hard cuts (good for talk content). 2 s is the sweet spot for shuffled music libraries. 5+ gives an overlapping radio-DJ feel. 10 is the cap.
